## Peer-review evidence

Peer review is assessed separately from discoverability in Google Scholar. Journal research-article labels, publisher editorial policies, and institutional peer-review classifications support the entries above.

- Springer research-article policy covers Jarvie and Rogers: https://link.springer.com/brands/springer/journal-policies . Article pages identify them as original research/papers.
- Management Science submission and editorial guidance covers Reagans and Huckman et al.: https://pubsonline.informs.org/page/mnsc/submission-guidelines .
- M&SOM explicitly describes its peer-review process: https://pubsonline.informs.org/page/msom/editorial-mission .
- Staats article publisher record includes research-article status; its Wiley record reports acceptance after revision: https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/10.1111/j.1937-5956.2011.01254.x .
- Nature Human Behaviour policies: https://www.nature.com/nathumbehav/editorial-policies/peer-review .
- Administrative Science Quarterly reviewer and submission policies: https://journals.sagepub.com/author-instructions/asq .
- Psychological Science explicitly identifies itself as peer reviewed: https://www.psychologicalscience.org/publications/psychological_science/ps-submissions .
- Papps author-institution record explicitly classifies the published article as peer-review (link in table).
